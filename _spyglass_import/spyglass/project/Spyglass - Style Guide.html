Color

Color in Spyglass is actor-semantic. Every surface, tag, and accent answers one question: whose hand is this? The palette is deliberately small — ink, gold, navy, and the paper neutrals — so that meaning, not decoration, drives every choice.

Usage rules
Reach for gold when
  • A surface or action belongs to the Manager
  • Content was AI-generated (use the ✦ conic chip)
  • You're marking a "client-ready" / success state
  • A brand moment needs warmth (the gold-housing mark)
Hold back when
  • It's a generic neutral surface — use paper
  • The action is a default primary — use navy
  • You'd be setting small text in raw gold (fails AA)
  • More than ~10% of a screen would turn gold

Motion
Easing

--ease: cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)

A decisive ease-out — fast to start, gentle to settle. Used on every hover lift, tab switch, and card transition. The signature 2px upward nudge on hover (translateY(-2px)) plus a shadow bloom.

Duration

--dur-fast 0.18s — taps, mono buttons
--dur 0.25s — cards, hovers
--dur-slow 0.6s — entrances

Always honor prefers-reduced-motion; animations collapse to near-zero.

06

Voice & patterns

Spyglass speaks like a discreet partner at a search firm: precise, calm, never hyped. It names things plainly, trusts white space, and lets the work be the proof. The writing earns trust the way the design does — by leaving things out.

We sound like
  • "Full strategy — never leaves the building."
  • "The redacted, outward-facing slice."
  • "The gate before the client."
  • Plain nouns: Matrix, dossier, pipeline, gate
We never sound like
  • "Revolutionary AI-powered hiring!"
  • "Supercharge your recruiting workflow 🚀"
  • Exclamation marks, hype, emoji
  • Jargon where a plain word works
Eyebrows · the mono layer talks
Short, uppercase, wide-tracked — they frame, they don't shout
✦ Single source of truth The fork is the whole product What carries through
Editing principle
One thousand no's for every yes

If a number, icon, or section isn't earning its place, cut it. The product's credibility comes from restraint — a sanitized shortlist, a clean grid, a single gold accent. When in doubt, leave it out.
