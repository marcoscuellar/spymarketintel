The fork is the whole product

One record, two faces. The Master Matrix is generated once, then projected into a Recruiter copy (full strategy) and a Candidate-facing copy. Same source — different audiences see different fields.

What carries through

The Matrix signals follow every candidate — from the pipeline, into the AI dossier, through the manager's gate, and onto the client's grid.
